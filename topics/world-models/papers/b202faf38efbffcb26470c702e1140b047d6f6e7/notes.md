# Paper notes — V-JEPA 2

## Summary

Successor to V-JEPA: self-supervised video models for **understanding, prediction, and planning**—extends JEPA line toward embodied action.

## Key claims

- Strong video understanding and planning from feature prediction objective

- Compositional generalization and FLOPs-matched baselines still absent

## Limitations
