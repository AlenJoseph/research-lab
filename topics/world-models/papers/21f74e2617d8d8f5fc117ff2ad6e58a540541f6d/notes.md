# Paper notes — SCAN compositional semantic parsing

## Summary

Benchmarks **compositional generalization** in semantic parsing: pre-training vs specialized architectures on SCAN and related splits.

## Key claims

- MLM pre-training can rival SCAN-specific architectures on primitive holdout splits
- Standard text benchmark family for systematic generalization

## Relevance

- **Primary text testbed** for compositional intuitive prediction (E1 language track)
- Cite for professor brief: composition literature is language-heavy, not vision-only

## Limitations

- Does not evaluate JEPA/latent reasoning or FLOPs-matched CoT baselines
