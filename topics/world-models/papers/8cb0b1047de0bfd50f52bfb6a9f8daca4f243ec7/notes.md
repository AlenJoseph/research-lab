# Paper notes — Compositional Generalization from First Principles

## Summary

Formal/principled treatment of **compositional generalization** requirements for neural systems—what it means to recombine known primitives systematically.

## Key claims

- Compositionality is not automatic from standard training; architectural and data assumptions matter
- Provides framework to design/evaluate systematic generalization

## Relevance to our problem/gap

- **Eval design** for Gap 1—systematic vs random splits, compositional gap metric
- Not integrated with JEPA training in this paper

## Limitations

- Theory/framework; empirical JEPA+composition experiments still needed
