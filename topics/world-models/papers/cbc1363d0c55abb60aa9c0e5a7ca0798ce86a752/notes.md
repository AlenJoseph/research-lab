# Paper notes — Scaling Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning

## Summary

Recurrent depth approach to scale **test-time compute** via **latent reasoning** loops instead of longer token chains.

## Key claims

- Test-time compute can be spent in latent space (recurrent depth) not only more tokens
- High citation traction in 2025 latent-reasoning literature

## Relevance

- Directly addresses **inference cost** of reasoning—core to intuition vs exhaustive context
- Must compare at matched FLOPs on compositional language tasks

## Limitations

- General reasoning benchmarks; systematic compositional splits not primary focus
